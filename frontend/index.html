Model notes

Surface sound speed 343 m/s. Ascent boom sourced at SLC-4E (v0 approximation of the sweeping boom carpet). Descent boom sourced at LZ-4, conditioned on an RTLS recovery, which is unconfirmed for these missions. Windows include profile slack (ascent −60/+180 s, descent −120/+240 s around Mach crossover) plus propagation delay from source to your location. Atmospheric refraction and winds can shift arrival by tens of seconds — treat windows as expectations, not appointments. Calibration anchor: Ventura, 133 km from LZ-4, observed boom ≈ T+8 min.
